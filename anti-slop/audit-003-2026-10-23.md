## Numbered Findings

### 1. Hero Ornamental Eyebrow Dividers (R-05 & taste-skill House Rules)

- **Priority**: MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)
- **Location**: `src/features/home/components/HomeHero.astro` lines 53–57
- **Current Code**:

  ```astro
  <div class="flex items-center justify-center gap-4">
    <span class="bg-orange-400/60 block-px inline-8 sm:inline-12"></span>
    <p class="text-xs font-semibold tracking-[0.2em] text-orange-300 uppercase sm:tracking-[0.3em]">
      {t("home.title")}
    </p>
    <span class="bg-orange-400/60 block-px inline-8 sm:inline-12"></span>
  </div>
  ```

- **Reason**: Ornamental divider lines framing an uppercase eyebrow label is a recognizable
  AI-template tell. It adds visual clutter above the headline without delivering substantive
  information.
- **Proposed Fix**: Replace the flanked lines with a clean, authentic location pill or integrate the
  destination badge naturally into the headline rhythm without decorative flanking bars.

### 2. Full-Viewport Triple Dark Scrim Muddying Landscape Photography (R-01 & R-25)

- **Priority**: HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Location**: `src/features/home/components/HomeHero.astro` line 48 &
  `src/shared/layout/PageHeader.astro` line 57
- **Current Code**:

  ```html
  <div class="absolute inset-0 z-10 bg-linear-to-b from-black/60 via-black/30 to-black/70"></div>
  ```

- **Reason**: Layering three heavy black stops across the entire 100vh image obscures the natural
  warmth and golden sunlight of authentic Moroccan Sahara/Atlas photography.
- **Proposed Fix**: Use a targeted bottom-to-top gradient scrim
  (`bg-gradient-to-t from-black/80 via-black/25 to-transparent`) positioned specifically behind the
  copy block, allowing the sky, dunes, and peaks in the upper 60% of the viewport to remain vibrant
  and crisp.

### 3. Rotating Headline Loop with Potential CLS & Delayed Message (R-07 & Emil Kowalski Principles)

- **Priority**: MEDIUM (Purpose-Gate)
- **Location**: `src/features/home/components/HomeHero.astro` lines 63–80
- **Current Code**: Rotating phrase carousel with invisible placeholder span
  (`#hero-desktop-placeholder`) to reserve width.
- **Reason**: Rotating hero text introduces layout shift risks prior to JS execution and delays
  communicating the core value proposition. Readers spend 5 seconds waiting for the full message
  rather than grasping what AmsirarTrip offers instantly.
- **Proposed Fix**: Present a bold, confident two-line statement (such as _"Private Sahara
  Expeditions & Handcrafted Berber Journeys"_) with instant comprehension, moving the secondary
  destinations into an interactive route badge or sub-caption.

---

### 4. Hardcoded Inline Hex/RGBA Values in Header Gradients (R-06 & Design System Invariant)

- **Priority**: LOW (Quality Lock)
- **Location**: `src/shared/layout/PageHeader.astro` lines 29–34
- **Current Code**:

  ```astro
  let gradientValue = "bg-[radial-gradient(circle_at_top,rgba(249,115,22,0.35),transparent_65%)]";
  ```

- **Reason**: Bypasses the centralized design token system (`--color-orange` / `--color-accent` in
  OKLCH) defined in `src/styles/global.css` and `DESIGN.md`.
- **Proposed Fix**: Standardize to semantic token classes or CSS variable references
  (`color-mix(in oklch, var(--color-orange) 35%, transparent)`).

---

### 5. Mobile Navigation Indicator Reduced-Motion Safeguard (R-04 & WCAG 2.2)

- **Priority**: HIGH (Hard Gate)
- **Location**: `src/shared/layout/navbar/MobileBottomNav.astro` lines 57–61
- **Current Code**:

  ```html
  <div
    class="pointer-events-none absolute inset-y-1.5 left-0 z-0 rounded-full bg-linear-to-br from-amber-orange to-orange opacity-0 shadow-lg shadow-orange/30 will-change-transform"
    id="mobile-nav-indicator"
  ></div>
  ```

- **Reason**: Sliding indicator animation lacks an explicit
  `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` style or class hook to switch from sliding translation
  to an instant opacity switch for users with vestibular sensitivities.
- **Proposed Fix**: Add `motion-reduce:transition-none motion-reduce:transform-none` and switch
  indicator state via immediate opacity for reduced-motion preferences.
