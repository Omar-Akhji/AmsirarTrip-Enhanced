## Numbered Findings

### 1. Excessive Border Radius on Dialog & Pill Inputs (R-04)

- **Priority**: HIGH
- **Location**: `NewsletterModal.vue` lines 87, 156, 181
- **Reason**: The dialog uses `rounded-3xl` and both the Name and Email fields use `rounded-full`,
  creating the stereotypical pill-shaped AI-template look instead of a disciplined radius scale
  (moderate `rounded-2xl` for dialog, `rounded-xl` for input fields).
- **Proposed Fix**: Set dialog to `rounded-2xl` with a crisp outer boundary, and change inputs from
  `rounded-full` to disciplined `rounded-xl` with warm stone borders
  (`border-stone-200 focus:border-orange-500`).

### 2. Sterile Default Container Lacking Brand Soul (R-05)

- **Priority**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: `NewsletterModal.vue` lines 90-101
- **Reason**: The dialog interior is a flat stark-white box with generic gray borders
  (`border-neutral-200 bg-white`), devoid of the Moroccan travel warmth and tactile character
  established by Archetype E.
- **Proposed Fix**: Introduce subtle warm paper/stone grounding (`bg-[#faf9f6]` or delicate top
  accent border in brand terracotta `#f97316`) and an optional delicate horse-head icon watermark or
  warm overline badge ("Sahara Dispatches · Marrakech").

### 3. Placeholder Dashed Border on reCAPTCHA Container (R-07)

- **Priority**: MEDIUM
- **Location**: `NewsletterModal.vue` line 188
- **Reason**: `border-dashed border-neutral-200` communicates an unfinished wireframe/placeholder
  rather than a finished production interface.
- **Proposed Fix**: Replace `border-dashed` with a clean, recessed container
  (`bg-stone-50/80 border border-stone-200/60 rounded-xl`).

### 4. Generic Header Composition & Missing Contextual Overline (R-12)

- **Priority**: LOW
- **Location**: `NewsletterModal.vue` lines 102-113
- **Reason**: The header is a plain stacked title + paragraph with no hierarchy anchors or brand
  specificity.
- **Proposed Fix**: Add a subtle, refined mono overline tag (`Amsirar Trip · Private Dispatches`)
  above the title and refine line heights for human typographic rhythm.

### 5. Input Icon Positioning & Focus Ring Polish (R-15)

- **Priority**: LOW
- **Location**: `NewsletterModal.vue` lines 142-184
- **Reason**: Icon colors use cold `text-neutral-400` and inputs use raw focus rings without
  transition smoothing.
- **Proposed Fix**: Warm the icon colors (`text-stone-400`), warm the placeholder colors
  (`placeholder:text-stone-400`), and tighten the focus ring tokens.
