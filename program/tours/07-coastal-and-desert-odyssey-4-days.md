## Overview

This four-day overland route connects the Atlantic coast of Agadir and Taghazout to the Sahara dunes of Merzouga and finishes in Marrakech. It is an ideal option if you are wrapping up a beach stay on the coast and want to experience the desert without flying or backtracking.

Leaving Agadir, you travel east past the red-walled market town of Taroudant and across the Anti-Atlas Mountains and saffron-growing plains of Taliouine. Passing Ouarzazate and the Valley of Roses, you spend Night 1 in the Dades Valley before visiting Todra Gorges and riding camels into the Erg Chebbi dunes on Day 2.

On the return leg, you drive through the palm groves of the Draa Valley to Ouarzazate for Night 3, then visit Kasbah Ait Ben Haddou and cross the High Atlas Mountains into Marrakech on Day 4.

> **Pricing Note:** Includes private A/C transport from Agadir to Marrakech, licensed driver-guide, 2 nights in boutique riads/hotels (Dades Valley and Ouarzazate), 1 night in an Erg Chebbi desert camp, camel rides, dinners, and daily breakfasts from Day 2.

## Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1: Agadir ➜ Taroudant ➜ Ouarzazate ➜ Dades Valley

- Early morning pick-up from your hotel or resort in Agadir or Taghazout (around 7:30 AM).
- Pass by Taroudant, known as "Little Marrakech" for its mud-brick ramparts and lively souks.
- Drive across the Anti-Atlas Mountains and Taliouine toward Ouarzazate.
- Continue through the Skoura palm oasis and Kalaat M'gouna (Valley of Roses) to reach the Dades Valley and its "Monkey Fingers" rock formations (~7 to 8 hours total driving).
- Dinner and overnight at a traditional riad or hotel in the Dades Valley.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Hotel or Riad in Dades Valley
- **Meals Included:** Dinner

### Day 2: Dades Valley ➜ Todra Gorges ➜ Merzouga Desert

- After breakfast, visit Todra Gorges to walk beneath its towering 300-meter canyon cliffs.
- Continue east via Tinejdad and Erfoud, known for fossils and dates.
- Arrive in Merzouga in the late afternoon and ride camels into the Erg Chebbi dunes for sunset.
- Enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner around the campfire with live Berber drumming and a night under the stars.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Desert Camp in Merzouga (Standard or Luxury)
- **Meals Included:** Dinner & Breakfast
- **Activities:** Sunset Camel Ride, Sandboarding, Campfire Music

### Day 3: Merzouga ➜ Alnif ➜ Draa Valley ➜ Ouarzazate

- Wake early to watch the sunrise over the sand dunes, then ride camels (or take a 4x4) back to Merzouga village for breakfast and a shower.
- Drive through Alnif and Tazzarine, following the Draa Valley—lined with dense palm groves and old kasbahs.
- Arrive in Ouarzazate in the late afternoon, with an optional visit to Atlas Film Studios or Taourirt Kasbah.
- Dinner and overnight at a traditional riad or hotel in Ouarzazate.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Hotel or Riad in Ouarzazate
- **Meals Included:** Dinner & Breakfast

### Day 4: Ouarzazate ➜ Ait Ben Haddou ➜ High Atlas Mountains ➜ Marrakech

- After breakfast in Ouarzazate, visit the famous UNESCO World Heritage Kasbah of Ait Ben Haddou.
- Cross the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n'Tichka Pass (2,260m) with scenic photo stops along the way.
- Arrive in Marrakech in the late afternoon (~4.5 hours driving), with drop-off at your riad, hotel, or the airport.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast
- **Arrival Time:** Between 5:00 PM and 6:00 PM

## Frequently Asked Questions

#### Q: Can you pick us up from our surf camp or hotel in Taghazout?
Yes. Your private driver will pick you up directly from your hotel, resort, or villa in Agadir, Taghazout, or Tamraght at around 7:30 AM on Day 1.

#### Q: Is this a practical way to travel from Agadir to Marrakech?
Yes. Rather than taking a direct highway transfer or domestic flight, this route turns your transfer into a full southern Morocco road trip through Taroudant, the Anti-Atlas, the Sahara, and the High Atlas.

#### Q: How are the road conditions between Agadir and Merzouga?
The entire route follows well-maintained paved roads. Our modern air-conditioned 4x4s and minivans ensure a smooth ride across mountain passes and desert plains with regular rest stops.

#### Q: Where do you drop us off in Marrakech?
We drop you directly at your Marrakech hotel, as close as vehicles can reach to your medina riad, or at Marrakech Menara Airport (RAK) by late afternoon on Day 4.
