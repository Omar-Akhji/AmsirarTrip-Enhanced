## Overview

This ten-day route links Morocco's High Atlas Mountains, the Sahara Desert, all four imperial cities (Marrakech, Fes, Meknes, and Rabat), the blue mountain town of Chefchaouen, and the Atlantic coast in one continuous trip.

Starting in Marrakech, you cross the Tizi n'Tichka pass to visit Kasbah Ait Ben Haddou and the Dades Valley before reaching the Erg Chebbi dunes in Merzouga for a camel ride and a night in a desert camp. From the Sahara, you drive north through the Middle Atlas cedar forests to Fes for a full-day walking tour of its old medina.

The final stretch takes you through the Roman ruins of Volubilis, imperial Meknes, and the blue-washed streets of Chefchaouen. You then head down to the coast to explore Rabat's historic monuments before concluding in Casablanca.

> **Pricing Note:** Includes private A/C transport for 10 days, licensed driver-guide, 8 nights in boutique riads and hotels, 1 night in an Erg Chebbi desert camp (standard or luxury upgrade available), licensed city guides in Marrakech and Fes, camel rides, dinners in Dades and the desert, and daily breakfasts from Day 2.

## Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1: Arrival in Marrakech

- Pick-up from Marrakech Menara Airport (RAK) and transfer to your riad or hotel in the Medina.
- Free time to explore Jemaa el-Fna square and the surrounding markets as street performers and food stalls set up at sunset.
- Optional dinner at a traditional Moroccan restaurant.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Riad or Hotel in Marrakech
- **Meals Included:** None

### Day 2: Marrakech: Guided City Tour

- Meet your licensed local guide after breakfast for a walking tour of Marrakech's landmarks: Koutoubia Mosque (exterior), Bahia Palace, and the Saadian Tombs.
- Walk through the historic souks selling spices, leatherwork, and brass lanterns. Optional afternoon visit to Majorelle Garden.
- Free afternoon to relax at your riad, visit a traditional hammam, or enjoy a rooftop café.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Riad or Hotel in Marrakech
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast

### Day 3: Marrakech ➜ Ait Ben Haddou ➜ Ouarzazate ➜ Dades Valley

- Leave Marrakech in the morning and cross the High Atlas Mountains over the Tizi n’Tichka Pass (2,260m).
- Stop at Kasbah Ait Ben Haddou, a UNESCO-listed fortified village made of rammed earth.
- Continue to Ouarzazate, with an optional stop at the Atlas Film Studios.
- Drive through the Skoura palm oasis and the Valley of Roses to reach the Dades Valley.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Hotel or Riad in Dades Valley
- **Meals Included:** Dinner & Breakfast

### Day 4: Dades Valley ➜ Todra Gorges ➜ Merzouga Desert

- After breakfast, head to Todra Gorges to walk between 300-meter vertical canyon walls.
- Continue via Tinejdad and Erfoud, known for fossils and date palms.
- Reach Merzouga in the late afternoon and ride camels across the Erg Chebbi dunes for sunset, dinner, and an overnight stay at your camp.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Desert Camp in Merzouga (Standard or Luxury)
- **Meals Included:** Dinner & Breakfast
- **Activities:** Sunset Camel Ride, Sandboarding, Campfire Music

### Day 5: Merzouga ➜ Ziz Valley ➜ Midelt ➜ Fes

- Wake before dawn to watch the sunrise over the dunes, then return to Merzouga village for breakfast and a shower.
- Drive north past Rissani and through the date-palm oasis of the Ziz Valley.
- Cross the Middle Atlas Mountains, stopping in Midelt for lunch, the Azrou cedar forest to see wild Barbary macaques, and alpine Ifrane.
- Arrive in Fes by early evening (~7 hours total driving).

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Riad in Fes
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast

### Day 6: Guided Tour of Fes

- Meet your licensed local guide for a full-day walking tour of Fes el-Bali, the oldest walled part of the city.
- Visit Al-Qarawiyyin University, Bou Inania Madrasa, Nejjarine Fountain, and the Chouara Tanneries.
- Explore the artisan quarters and bustling medina markets.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Riad in Fes
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast

### Day 7: Fes ➜ Meknes ➜ Volubilis ➜ Chefchaouen

- Depart for Meknes to see the monumental Bab Mansour Gate and El Hedim Square.
- Continue to the Roman ruins of Volubilis to walk among preserved mosaics and stone arches.
- Drive north into the Rif Mountains to reach Chefchaouen (~4 hours from Volubilis).

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Riad or Hotel in Chefchaouen
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast

### Day 8: Chefchaouen: Free Day

- A full free day to enjoy Chefchaouen at your own pace.
- Walk the blue-painted alleys, browse local weaving shops, or hike up to the Spanish Mosque for sunset views over the town.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Riad or Hotel in Chefchaouen
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast

### Day 9: Chefchaouen ➜ Rabat ➜ Casablanca

- After breakfast, drive down from the mountains to Rabat, Morocco’s capital.
- Visit the Hassan Tower, Mohammed V Mausoleum, and the Kasbah of the Udayas overlooking the ocean.
- Continue south along the highway to Casablanca.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Hotel in Casablanca
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast

### Day 10: Casablanca: Departure

- Breakfast at your hotel, with an optional morning visit to the Hassan II Mosque (depending on your flight time).
- Private transfer to Casablanca Mohammed V Airport (CMN) for your flight home.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast

## Frequently Asked Questions

#### Q: Why does the tour start in Marrakech and end in Casablanca?
Casablanca Mohammed V Airport (CMN) handles the majority of long-haul international flights, making it a convenient exit point without backtracking to Marrakech. However, we can easily run this route in reverse or loop back to Marrakech if your flights require it.

#### Q: Are local guides included in the imperial cities?
Yes. Official licensed guides are included for dedicated walking tours in both Marrakech and Fes so you can navigate the souks easily and learn the history behind each landmark.

#### Q: Is the driving pace exhausting over 10 days?
No. We intentionally build in two-night stays in Marrakech, Fes, and Chefchaouen so you aren't packing your bags every morning. Vehicles are late-model, air-conditioned, and equipped with Wi-Fi and bottled water.

#### Q: Can we choose our hotel comfort level?
Yes. We offer three accommodation tiers: Authentic Heritage (traditional riads), Superior Comfort (boutique riads and 4-star hotels), and Luxury VIP (5-star properties and luxury desert tents with private bathrooms).

#### Q: How much luggage can we bring?
Because the vehicle is private for your group, there is plenty of trunk space for full-size suitcases and carry-on bags.
