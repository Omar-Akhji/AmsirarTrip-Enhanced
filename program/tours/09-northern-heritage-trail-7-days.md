## Overview

This seven-day overland journey is built for travelers arriving in Tangier—whether by ferry from Spain or by air—who want to travel all the way south to Marrakech. Starting where the Atlantic meets the Mediterranean at Cap Spartel, you explore Tangier's old medina before heading into the Rif Mountains via Tetouan to spend a night in the blue-painted town of Chefchaouen.

From the mountains, you visit the Roman ruins of Volubilis and the royal stables of imperial Meknes on your way to Fes, where you spend two nights and take a full-day guided walking tour through its 1,200-year-old medina.

The final stretch takes you south across the Middle Atlas cedar forests, stopping to see wild Barbary macaques in Azrou before descending into Marrakech for two nights and a private guided tour of the Red City.

## Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1: Arrival in Tangier: City Highlights

- Pick-up from Tangier Ibn Battouta Airport (TNG), Tangier City Port (ferries from Tarifa), or Tanger Med Port.
- Visit Cap Spartel, where the Atlantic Ocean meets the Mediterranean Sea, and explore the Caves of Hercules.
- Walk through Tangier's historic Kasbah, the winding old medina, and the lively Petit Socco square.
- Stroll along the beach promenade before checking into your hotel or riad.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Hotel or Riad in Tangier
- **Meals Included:** None

### Day 2: Tangier ➜ Tetouan ➜ Chefchaouen

- After breakfast, drive through the Rif foothills to Tetouan ("The White Dove") to walk through its Andalusian-influenced old medina.
- Continue into the mountains to Chefchaouen, Morocco's famous blue city.
- Spend the afternoon exploring Outa El Hammam square, Ras El Ma stream, and local artisan shops, followed by a sunset walk up to the Spanish Mosque viewpoint.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Hotel or Riad in Chefchaouen
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast

### Day 3: Chefchaouen ➜ Volubilis ➜ Meknes ➜ Fes

- Enjoy a quiet morning in Chefchaouen before departing south.
- Stop at Volubilis (~2.5 hours drive) to explore Morocco's best-preserved Roman ruins and floor mosaics.
- Continue to Meknes to see the Bab Mansour Gate, Sahrij Swani (Royal Stables), and the old medina.
- Arrive in Fes by early evening.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Hotel or Riad in Fes
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast

### Day 4: Fes: Full-Day Medina Tour

- Meet your licensed local guide after breakfast for a full-day walking tour of Fes el-Bali.
- Visit Al-Qarawiyyin University (founded in 859 CE), Bou Inania Madrasa, Attarine Madrasa, and the Nejjarine Wooden Arts Museum.
- Watch the leather dyers at work from a terrace overlooking the Chouara Tanneries and visit traditional pottery and zellige tile workshops.
- Free evening to relax at your riad or enjoy a traditional dinner.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Hotel or Riad in Fes
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast

### Day 5: Fes ➜ Ifrane ➜ Azrou ➜ Beni Mellal ➜ Marrakech

- Leave Fes and drive into the Middle Atlas Mountains, stopping in alpine Ifrane.
- Stop at the Azrou Cedar Forest to observe wild Barbary macaques in the trees.
- Continue south through the Tadla plains and Beni Mellal (~7 hours total driving).
- Arrive in Marrakech in the early evening and check into your riad in the Medina.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Hotel or Riad in Marrakech
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast

### Day 6: Full Day Exploring Marrakech

- Meet your licensed local guide for a walking tour of Marrakech: Koutoubia Mosque (exterior), Bahia Palace, and the Saadian Tombs.
- Walk through the historic Mellah (Jewish Quarter), the Medina alleys, and the colorful souks leading to Jemaa el-Fna square.
- Optional afternoon visit to Majorelle Garden and the Yves Saint Laurent Museum.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Hotel or Riad in Marrakech
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast

### Day 7: Marrakech: Departure

- Breakfast at your riad and free time depending on your flight schedule.
- Private transfer to Marrakech Menara Airport (RAK) for your departure flight.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast

## Good to Know & Practical Advice

- **Medina Access:** Cars cannot enter the narrow pedestrian alleys of Chefchaouen, Fes, or Marrakech; your driver will park at the nearest gate and arrange assistance with your luggage to your riad.
- **Footwear:** Bring comfortable walking shoes—Chefchaouen and Fes have steep, cobbled alleys.
- **Weather:** Northern Morocco and the Middle Atlas are noticeably cooler than Marrakech, especially between November and March.
- **Flexibility:** We can add a night in the Sahara Desert (Merzouga) between Fes and Marrakech if you extend the trip to 8 or 9 days.

## Frequently Asked Questions

#### Q: Can you pick us up directly from the ferry port in Tangier?
Yes. Whether you arrive by fast ferry from Tarifa at Tangier City Port, via Tanger Med Port, or at Tangier Airport (TNG), your driver will meet you right outside customs with a personalized sign.

#### Q: Is this a good route if we are coming from Spain?
Yes. It is the most logical north-to-south route through Morocco, allowing you to take the ferry across the Strait of Gibraltar, explore the north and the imperial cities, and fly home directly from Marrakech without backtracking.

#### Q: How long are the daily drives?
Most driving legs average 3 to 4.5 hours along good highways and scenic mountain roads. The only long travel day is Day 5 (Fes to Marrakech, ~7 hours), broken up with stops in Ifrane and the Azrou cedar forest.

#### Q: Are local city guides included in Fes and Marrakech?
Yes. Licensed local guides are included for dedicated walking tours in both Fes and Marrakech so you get rich historical insight and navigate the medinas effortlessly.

#### Q: Can we add a Sahara Desert night to this itinerary?
Yes. If you have 8 or 9 days available, we can route you from Fes through the Ziz Valley to Merzouga for a camel trek and desert camp stay before continuing to Marrakech.
