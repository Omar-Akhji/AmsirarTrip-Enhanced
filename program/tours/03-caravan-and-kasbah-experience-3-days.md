## Overview

This three-day point-to-point route connects Fes to Marrakech via the Sahara Desert, saving you from having to backtrack across the country. Leaving Fes in the morning, you cross the Middle Atlas Mountains through alpine Ifrane and the cedar forests of Azrou before descending the palm-filled Ziz Valley to Merzouga.

In Merzouga, you ride camels into the Erg Chebbi dunes for sunset and spend the night at a desert camp with dinner and local Berber music around the fire. 

On the final two days, the journey turns west through the 300-meter cliffs of Todra Gorges, the Dades Valley, and the UNESCO-listed Kasbah Ait Ben Haddou, finishing over the High Atlas Mountains in Marrakech.

> **Pricing Note:** Includes one-way private A/C transport from Fes to Marrakech, licensed driver-guide, 1 night in a Merzouga desert camp, 1 night in a Dades Valley hotel/riad, camel rides, dinners, and daily breakfasts from Day 2.

## Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1: Fes ➜ Ifrane ➜ Azrou ➜ Midelt ➜ Ziz Valley ➜ Merzouga

- Pick-up from your Fes riad or hotel around 7:30 AM.
- Head into the Middle Atlas Mountains, stopping in Ifrane, known for its clean streets and chalet-style architecture.
- Stop at the Azrou Cedar Forest to see the wild Barbary macaques.
- Continue to Midelt for lunch with views of the Ayachi mountain range.
- Drive through Errachidia and along the Ziz Valley gorge, lined with dense date palm groves.
- Arrive in Merzouga in the late afternoon and ride camels across the Erg Chebbi dunes to your camp for sunset and dinner.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Desert Camp in Merzouga (Standard or Luxury)
- **Meals Included:** Dinner
- **Activities:** Sunset Camel Ride, Berber Music, Sandboarding

### Day 2: Merzouga ➜ Erfoud ➜ Todra Gorges ➜ Dades Valley

- Wake early to watch the sunrise over the dunes, then ride camels back to Merzouga village for breakfast and a shower.
- Pass through Erfoud, famous for its fossil workshops and dates.
- Reach Tinghir and walk along the floor of Todra Gorges beneath 300-meter limestone walls.
- Continue to the Dades Valley to see the "Monkey Fingers" rock formations.
- Dinner and overnight stay at a hotel or riad overlooking the Dades Gorges.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Hotel or Riad in Dades Valley
- **Meals Included:** Dinner & Breakfast

### Day 3: Dades Valley ➜ Roses Valley ➜ Ouarzazate ➜ Ait Ben Haddou ➜ Marrakech

- After breakfast, drive through Kalaat M'gouna (Valley of Roses) and the Skoura palm oasis.
- Pass through Ouarzazate, with an optional stop at the Atlas Film Studios.
- Visit Kasbah Ait Ben Haddou, the famous fortified mud-brick village used in *Lawrence of Arabia* and *Game of Thrones*.
- Cross the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n'Tichka Pass (2,260m) with photo stops along the way.
- Arrive in Marrakech in the evening with drop-off at your accommodation.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast
- **Arrival Time:** Between 7:00 PM and 8:00 PM

## Frequently Asked Questions

#### Q: Is this a direct one-way trip from Fes to Marrakech?
Yes. We pick you up at your accommodation in Fes on Day 1 and drop you off at your hotel or riad in Marrakech on Day 3.

#### Q: How long is the drive on Day 1 from Fes to Merzouga?
It covers roughly 470 km (about 7.5 to 8 hours). The drive is paced with regular stops in Ifrane, the Azrou cedar forest, Midelt for lunch, and panoramic viewpoints over the Ziz Valley before reaching the dunes in the late afternoon.

#### Q: What happens to our large suitcases during the camel ride?
Your main luggage stays safely locked inside your private vehicle or is driven directly to the desert camp by 4x4 support. You only need to carry a small backpack on the camel.

#### Q: What is the difference between Standard and Luxury camps?
Standard camps provide private furnished tents with shared bathroom facilities. Luxury camps offer spacious tents with private en-suite hot showers, flush toilets, and charging outlets.

#### Q: Can you accommodate special dietary needs?
Yes. Vegetarian, vegan, and gluten-free meals are easily arranged at both the riad and the desert camp if mentioned at booking.
