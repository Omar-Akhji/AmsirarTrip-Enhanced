## Overview

This three-day trip connects Marrakech to the high orange dunes of Erg Chebbi in Merzouga. You travel in a private, air-conditioned vehicle across the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n'Tichka pass (2,260m), stopping at the earthen ksar of Ait Ben Haddou and Ouarzazate before spending the first night in the Dades Valley.

On the second day, the road passes through Tinghir to reach the 300-meter limestone walls of Todra Gorges. From there, you continue past Erfoud and the Tafilalet palm groves to Merzouga. Late in the afternoon, you ride camels into the dunes for sunset and arrive at your desert camp for dinner, local music around the fire, and a quiet night under the stars.

After watching the sunrise over the sand and having breakfast, you drive back to Marrakech through Alnif, the palm-lined Draa Valley, and Ouarzazate, crossing the High Atlas again to arrive by early evening.

> **Pricing Note:** Includes private A/C transport, licensed driver-guide, 1 night in a Dades Valley riad/hotel, 1 night in a desert camp, dinners, daily breakfasts starting Day 2, and camel rides. Luxury camp upgrades with private bathrooms are available on request.

## Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1: Marrakech ➜ Ait Ben Haddou ➜ Ouarzazate ➜ Dades Valley

- Leave Marrakech early in the morning and cross the High Atlas Mountains over the Tizi n’Tichka Pass (2,260m).
- Stop at Kasbah Ait Ben Haddou, a historic fortified village featured in *Gladiator* and *Game of Thrones*.
- Continue to Ouarzazate, known for its film studios and historic kasbahs.
- Drive through the Skoura palm oasis and the Valley of Roses to reach Dades Valley.
- Dinner and overnight stay at a local riad or hotel in the valley.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Hotel or Riad in Dades Valley
- **Meals Included:** Dinner

### Day 2: Dades Valley ➜ Todra Gorges ➜ Merzouga

- After breakfast, head toward Todra Gorges to walk along the riverbed beneath 300-meter cliffs.
- Continue through Tinejdad and Erfoud, a town known for fossil workshops and date markets.
- Reach Merzouga in the late afternoon, where your camels wait at the edge of the sand.
- Ride into the Erg Chebbi dunes in time for sunset.
- Settle into your desert camp for a Moroccan dinner, Berber drumming by the fire, and an overnight stay.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Desert Camp in Merzouga (Standard or Luxury)
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast & Dinner
- **Activities:** Sunset Camel Ride & Campfire Music

### Day 3: Merzouga ➜ Alnif ➜ Draa Valley ➜ Ouarzazate ➜ Marrakech

- Wake early to watch the sunrise from the dunes near your camp.
- Ride camels (or take a 4x4 transfer) back to Merzouga village for breakfast and a shower.
- Drive back via Alnif and Tazzarine, joining the Draa Valley—a long river oasis lined with date palms and old mud-brick kasbahs.
- Stop in Ouarzazate for lunch, with optional time to visit Atlas Film Studios or Taourirt Kasbah.
- Cross the High Atlas Mountains via Tizi n'Tichka Pass (2,260m) and arrive back in Marrakech in the evening.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast
- **Arrival Time:** Between 7:00 PM and 8:00 PM

## Frequently Asked Questions

#### Q: Is this tour private or shared?
Most guests book this as a private tour, which lets you stop whenever you like for photos, coffee, or rest breaks. We also run small-group shared departures on select dates.

#### Q: How much driving is involved between Marrakech and Merzouga?
The round trip is roughly 1,120 km. Splitting it over three days keeps the pace manageable: Day 1 covers about 310 km to Dades Valley, Day 2 covers 250 km to Merzouga, and Day 3 returns via the Draa Valley and Ouarzazate.

#### Q: What are the desert camps like?
Standard camps offer private tents with real beds and shared bathrooms. Luxury camps provide larger private tents with en-suite hot showers, flush toilets, and power outlets. Both include dinner, breakfast, and evening music around the fire.

#### Q: Can we finish the tour in Fes instead of returning to Marrakech?
Yes. Days 1 and 2 stay the same. On Day 3, instead of turning west toward Ouarzazate, your driver heads north through the Ziz Valley, Midelt, and the Azrou cedar forest, dropping you in Fes by early evening.

#### Q: Do you accommodate vegetarian, vegan, or gluten-free diets?
Yes. Riads and desert camps regularly prepare vegetable tagines, couscous, and fresh salads. Let us know your dietary needs when booking so we can inform the kitchens ahead of time.
