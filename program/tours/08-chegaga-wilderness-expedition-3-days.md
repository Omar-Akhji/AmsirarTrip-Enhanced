## Overview

This three-day expedition takes you far off the paved tourist trail into Erg Chegaga, Morocco's largest and wildest dune system. Unlike Merzouga, which sits right beside a paved road, Erg Chegaga lies 60 km across open desert tracks and can only be reached by 4x4.

Leaving Marrakech, you cross the High Atlas via Tizi n'Tichka and follow the entire length of the Draa Valley—a massive oasis lined with over a million date palms and crumbling kasbahs—to spend Night 1 in Zagora. On Day 2, you drive past M'Hamid El Ghizlane, where the asphalt ends, navigating rocky plateaus and dry riverbeds by 4x4 into the 300-meter dunes of Erg Chegaga.

After a sunset camel ride and a quiet night in a remote desert bivouac far from other camps, you complete a scenic loop back to Marrakech across the dry salt flats of Lake Iriqui, Foum Zguid, and the carpet-weaving town of Taznakht.

## Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1: Marrakech ➜ Ait Ben Haddou ➜ Ouarzazate ➜ Zagora

- Leave Marrakech early in the morning (around 7:30 AM) and cross the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n'Tichka Pass (2,260m).
- Stop at Kasbah Ait Ben Haddou, the famous UNESCO-listed earthen village.
- Continue past Ouarzazate and follow the Draa Valley—Morocco's longest river oasis—south to Zagora (~7 hours total driving).
- Dinner and overnight at a traditional riad or hotel in Zagora.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Hotel or Riad in Zagora
- **Meals Included:** Dinner

### Day 2: Zagora ➜ M'Hamid ➜ Erg Chegaga Desert

- After breakfast, head south through Tamgroute (known for its green pottery and historic Quranic library) to M'Hamid, the last village before the open desert.
- Leave the paved road behind for a 60 km off-road 4x4 drive across sand flats and stony desert (*reg*) to reach the tall dunes of Erg Chegaga.
- Ride camels into the dunes for sunset before returning to camp for a traditional dinner around the fire, nomad drumming, and stargazing under pitch-black skies.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Desert Camp in Erg Chegaga
- **Meals Included:** Dinner & Breakfast
- **Activities:** Off-Road 4x4 Track, Sunset Camel Ride, Campfire Music, Stargazing

### Day 3: Erg Chegaga ➜ Lake Iriqui ➜ Foum Zguid ➜ Taznakht ➜ Marrakech

- Wake early to watch the sunrise over the dunes, followed by breakfast at the camp.
- Depart off-road by 4x4 across the flat, dry lakebed of Lake Iriqui and fossil-rich plains toward Foum Zguid.
- Rejoin the paved road and continue through Taznakht, famous for hand-woven Berber rugs, where you can stop for lunch.
- Cross the High Atlas Mountains again via Tizi n'Tichka Pass (2,260m) to arrive back in Marrakech in the evening (~8 hours total driving).

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast
- **Arrival Time:** Late afternoon / early evening in Marrakech

## Frequently Asked Questions

#### Q: What is the difference between Erg Chegaga and Merzouga (Erg Chebbi)?
Merzouga is easily reached by paved road and has dozens of hotels and camps right at the edge of the dunes. Erg Chegaga is wider, wilder, and requires a 60 km off-road 4x4 drive across open desert tracks, offering far fewer crowds and a true wilderness feel.

#### Q: Is a 4x4 vehicle guaranteed for this trip?
Yes. Minibuses cannot cross the sand and rocky tracks between M'Hamid, Erg Chegaga, and Lake Iriqui. All Chegaga expeditions are operated exclusively in private, air-conditioned 4x4 Toyota Land Cruisers (or equivalent) with licensed desert drivers.

#### Q: What is the return route like on Day 3?
Instead of backtracking through Zagora, you drive west across the dry salt flats of Lake Iriqui to Foum Zguid, then north through Taznakht and over the High Atlas Mountains, forming a complete desert loop.

#### Q: What facilities are at the Chegaga desert camp?
Standard camps feature private nomad tents with proper beds, clean linens, and shared bathrooms. Luxury camp upgrades include private en-suite hot showers, flush toilets, and solar power outlets.

#### Q: When is the best time of year for Erg Chegaga?
October through May offers the most pleasant weather, with sunny daytime temperatures around 20°C–28°C (68°F–82°F) and crisp nights. July and August are extremely hot in the deep desert.
