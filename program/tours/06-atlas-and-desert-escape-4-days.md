## Overview

This four-day route gives you the full Marrakech-to-Merzouga desert experience at a much calmer pace than the standard three-day trip. Spreading the 1,100 km round trip across four days keeps daily driving legs between 4 and 6 hours, leaving more time to walk through Berber villages, visit kasbahs, and relax at your riads.

Crossing the High Atlas via the Tizi n'Tichka pass, you visit Kasbah Ait Ben Haddou before spending Night 1 in the Dades Valley. Day 2 takes you through the 300-meter cliffs of Todra Gorges to Merzouga for a sunset camel ride and an overnight stay in the Erg Chebbi dunes.

Instead of rushing straight back to Marrakech on Day 3, you drive through the Draa Valley palm groves to spend the night in Ouarzazate, leaving a relaxed, scenic drive over the High Atlas for Day 4.

> **Pricing Note:** Includes private A/C 4x4 or minivan transport, licensed driver-guide, 2 nights in boutique riads/hotels (Dades Valley and Ouarzazate), 1 night in an Erg Chebbi desert camp, camel rides, dinners, and daily breakfasts from Day 2.

## Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1: Marrakech ➜ Ait Ben Haddou ➜ Ouarzazate ➜ Dades Valley

- Leave Marrakech around 7:30 AM and cross the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n’Tichka Pass (2,260m), stopping at panoramic viewpoints.
- Explore Kasbah Ait Ben Haddou, the famous earthen citadel featured in *Gladiator* and *Game of Thrones*.
- Pass through Ouarzazate (optional visit to Atlas Film Studios), the Skoura palm oasis, and Kalaat M'gouna (Valley of Roses).
- Arrive in the Dades Valley to see the "Monkey Fingers" rock formations before dinner and overnight at your riad or hotel.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Hotel or Riad in Dades Valley
- **Meals Included:** Dinner

### Day 2: Dades Valley ➜ Todra Gorges ➜ Merzouga Desert

- After breakfast, drive to Todra Gorges to walk between its sheer 300-meter limestone walls.
- Continue through Tinejdad and Erfoud, known for fossil workshops and date groves.
- Arrive in Merzouga in the late afternoon, meet your camel caravan, and ride into the Erg Chebbi dunes for sunset.
- Enjoy a traditional Moroccan dinner, Berber drumming around the campfire, and a night under the desert sky.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Desert Camp in Merzouga (Standard or Luxury)
- **Meals Included:** Dinner & Breakfast
- **Activities:** Sunset Camel Ride, Sandboarding, Campfire Music

### Day 3: Merzouga ➜ Alnif ➜ Draa Valley ➜ Ouarzazate

- Wake early to watch the sunrise over the dunes, then ride camels (or take a 4x4) back to Merzouga village for breakfast and a shower.
- Drive west through Alnif and Tazzarine, joining the Draa Valley—Morocco’s longest river oasis, lined with date palms and ancient mud-brick kasbahs.
- Arrive in Ouarzazate in the late afternoon for dinner and overnight at a local riad or hotel.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Hotel or Riad in Ouarzazate
- **Meals Included:** Dinner & Breakfast

### Day 4: Ouarzazate ➜ High Atlas Mountains ➜ Marrakech

- After breakfast, visit the historic Taourirt Kasbah or Atlas Film Studios in Ouarzazate (optional).
- Optional brief photo stop near Ait Ben Haddou before ascending the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n'Tichka Pass (2,260m).
- Arrive back in Marrakech by late afternoon (~4.5 hours driving).

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast
- **Arrival Time:** Between 5:00 PM and 6:00 PM

## Frequently Asked Questions

#### Q: Why choose the 4-day tour instead of the 3-day option?
The 3-day itinerary requires a 9-to-10-hour drive on the final day from Merzouga back to Marrakech. Adding a fourth day breaks up that return leg with an overnight stay in Ouarzazate, keeping daily drives comfortable and giving you time to enjoy the Draa Valley.

#### Q: What accommodations are included?
You spend Night 1 in a riad or hotel in the Dades Valley, Night 2 in an Erg Chebbi desert camp, and Night 3 in a boutique riad or hotel in Ouarzazate. Luxury desert tents with private en-suite bathrooms are available on request.

#### Q: Can this 4-day trip end in Fes instead of Marrakech?
Yes. On Day 3, instead of driving west toward Ouarzazate, your driver can head north through the Ziz Valley, Midelt, and the Azrou cedar forest to drop you in Fes by early evening.

#### Q: Which meals are included?
All breakfasts (from Day 2) and all three evening dinners at your riads and desert camp are included. Lunches and drinks are at your own expense at scenic stops along the route.
