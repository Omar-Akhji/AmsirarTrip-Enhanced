## Overview

This six-day route connects Morocco's Atlantic coast, the Rif Mountains, two imperial capitals, and the Sahara Desert. Starting in Casablanca with a stop at the oceanfront Hassan II Mosque, you head north through Rabat before climbing into the blue-washed streets of Chefchaouen.

From the mountains, the road leads past the Roman ruins of Volubilis and the monumental gates of Meknes into Fes. You spend a full day walking the medieval alleys of Fes el-Bali with a licensed local guide, visiting historic madrasas, Al-Qarawiyyin University, and the Chouara leather tanneries.

The second half of the trip crosses the Middle Atlas cedar forests and the Ziz Valley down to the sand dunes of Merzouga. After a camel ride and a night in a Sahara camp, you travel west through Todra Gorges, the Dades Valley, and Kasbah Ait Ben Haddou, finishing in Marrakech.

> **Pricing Note:** Includes private A/C vehicle with driver-guide, 4 nights in boutique riads/hotels (1 night Chefchaouen, 2 nights Fes, 1 night Dades Valley), 1 night in an Erg Chebbi desert camp, a full-day licensed guide in Fes, camel rides, dinners in the desert and Dades, and daily breakfasts from Day 2.

## Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1: Casablanca ➜ Rabat ➜ Chefchaouen

- Morning pick-up from your Casablanca hotel or Mohammed V Airport.
- Visit the Hassan II Mosque, built right over the Atlantic Ocean.
- Drive north to Rabat to see the Hassan Tower, Mohammed V Mausoleum, and the Kasbah of the Udayas.
- Continue into the Rif Mountains to reach Chefchaouen by late afternoon.
- Free evening to wander the blue-painted medina at your own pace.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Hotel or Riad in Chefchaouen
- **Meals Included:** None

### Day 2: Chefchaouen ➜ Volubilis ➜ Meknes ➜ Fes

- Enjoy a relaxed morning in Chefchaouen for photos and quiet walks before the day-trippers arrive.
- Drive south to Volubilis to explore the preserved Roman ruins and floor mosaics.
- Stop in Meknes to see the Bab Mansour gate and El Hedim Square.
- Continue to Fes and check into your riad in the medina.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Riad in Fes Medina
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast

### Day 3: Full-Day Guided Tour of Fes

- Meet your licensed local guide after breakfast for a walking tour of Fes el-Bali.
- Visit Al-Qarawiyyin University, Bou Inania Madrasa, Nejjarine Fountain, and the Chouara Tanneries.
- Walk through the artisan quarters where weavers, coppersmiths, and potters still work by hand.
- Optional drive up to the Merenid Tombs for a panoramic view over the city before returning to your riad.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Riad in Fes Medina
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast

### Day 4: Fes ➜ Midelt ➜ Ziz Valley ➜ Merzouga Desert

- Leave early for the drive south across the Middle Atlas Mountains, passing Ifrane and the Azrou cedar forest (home to wild Barbary macaques).
- Stop in Midelt for lunch, then follow the Ziz River gorge lined with thousands of date palms.
- Arrive in Merzouga at the edge of the Erg Chebbi dunes in the late afternoon.
- Ride camels across the dunes to watch the sunset and spend the night at your desert camp.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Desert Camp in Merzouga (Standard or Luxury)
- **Meals Included:** Dinner & Breakfast
- **Activities:** Camel Ride, Sandboarding, Berber Music

### Day 5: Merzouga ➜ Todra Gorges ➜ Dades Valley

- Wake early for sunrise over the sand dunes, then ride back to Merzouga village for breakfast and a shower.
- Drive to Todra Gorges and walk between its 300-meter vertical canyon walls.
- Continue to the Dades Valley, stopping to see the "Monkey Fingers" rock formations.
- Dinner and overnight stay at a hotel or riad overlooking the valley.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Hotel or Riad in Dades Valley
- **Meals Included:** Dinner & Breakfast

### Day 6: Dades Valley ➜ Ouarzazate ➜ Ait Ben Haddou ➜ Marrakech

- After breakfast, pass through Kalaat M’gouna (Valley of Roses) and the Skoura palm oasis.
- Stop at Kasbah Ait Ben Haddou for a walk through this famous earthen citadel.
- Cross the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n’Tichka Pass (2,260m).
- Arrive in Marrakech in the early evening with drop-off at your riad or hotel.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast

## Frequently Asked Questions

#### Q: Can you pick us up directly from Casablanca Airport (CMN)?
Yes. Your driver will meet you in the arrivals hall at Mohammed V Airport with a name sign, or pick you up from your Casablanca hotel at your preferred time.

#### Q: Is a local guide included in Fes?
Yes. Because Fes el-Bali has over 9,000 narrow alleys, a licensed historical guide accompanies you for the day so you don't get lost and get the full context behind the monuments.

#### Q: Which day has the longest drive?
Day 4 (Fes to Merzouga) is the longest leg at around 7 hours of driving, broken up with stops in Ifrane, the Azrou monkey forest, Midelt for lunch, and viewpoints over the Ziz Valley.

#### Q: What accommodations are included?
You stay 1 night in Chefchaouen, 2 nights in Fes, 1 night in a Merzouga desert camp, and 1 night in Dades Valley. Luxury camp upgrades with private bathrooms and climate control are available.

#### Q: Can we customize this route?
Yes. Since this is a private tour, we can easily add a night in Essaouira at the end or adjust the pace to fit your flight schedule.
