## 1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide directly to us when planning or booking your
Moroccan journey, contacting our team, or subscribing to updates. This includes:

- **Contact and Identity Details**: Your full name, email address, telephone / WhatsApp number, and
  country of residence.
- **Booking & Travel Specifications**: Travel dates, preferred tour itineraries, party size, dietary
  requirements, and custom excursion requests.
- **Technical & Device Information**: IP address, device type, browser specification, and general
  geographic location collected through server logs and optional analytics.

We never collect sensitive personal categories (such as racial background, political opinions, or
religious beliefs) without your explicit consent or operational necessity.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We process your data strictly in accordance with international data protection standards (GDPR) and
Moroccan Law No. 09-08 (CNDP) for the following lawful purposes:

- **Contract Fulfillment**: To arrange your guided tours, desert camps, transportation, and hotel
  bookings.
- **Customer Care & Communication**: To answer booking inquiries, provide pre-departure briefings,
  and respond via email or WhatsApp helpline.
- **Service Optimization**: To analyze aggregate visitor trends, troubleshoot technical issues, and
  improve website navigation.
- **Legal Compliance**: To comply with Moroccan tourism regulations, tax requirements, and safety
  reporting.

We never sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties or data brokers.

## 3. Comprehensive Cookie Policy

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you browse websites. They
enable the website to remember your actions and preferences over a period of time, ensuring smooth
and secure operation.

### Legal Bases for Cookies

Under EU GDPR (Regulation 2016/679) and the ePrivacy Directive (Directive 2002/58/EC):

- **Strictly Necessary Cookies** are placed based on our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) and
  contractual necessity (Art. 6(1)(b)) to provide secure, core services you specifically requested.
- **Analytics and Functional Cookies** are placed exclusively upon your freely given, informed, and
  revocable consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).

### Browser-Level Controls

In addition to our consent manager, most modern web browsers allow you to control cookies through
their configuration settings:

- **Google Chrome**: Settings > Privacy and Security > Cookies and other site data.
- **Apple Safari**: Preferences > Privacy > Block all cookies.
- **Mozilla Firefox**: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data.
- **Microsoft Edge**: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies.

## 4. Data Security & Storage

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal
data against accidental loss, unauthorized access, alteration, and disclosure:

- **Encryption in Transit**: All web traffic is encrypted using modern TLS 1.3 cryptographic
  protocols with HTTPS enforcement.
- **Strict Content Security Policy (CSP)**: Nonce-based execution guards against Cross-Site
  Scripting (XSS) and code injection.
- **Access Restrictions**: Access to booking details is strictly limited to authorized personnel
  handling your travel arrangements.

## 5. Third-Party Service Providers

To operate our services effectively, we partner with reputable third-party processors who adhere to
stringent confidentiality and data protection standards:

- **Hosting & Infrastructure**: Hostinger Cloud VPS (European datacenters).
- **Transactional Email**: Google Workspace / Gmail SMTP for booking confirmations.
- **Interactive Mapping**: Google Maps API (only loaded upon explicit visitor interaction).

## 6. Your Rights Under GDPR & Moroccan Law

Depending on your jurisdiction, you have extensive legal rights regarding your personal data:

- **Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR)**: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
- **Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)**: Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete personal
  information.
- **Right to Erasure / To Be Forgotten (Art. 17 GDPR)**: Request deletion of your personal data when
  no longer needed for legal or contractual purposes.
- **Right to Restrict Processing (Art. 18 GDPR)**: Request limitation of how we process your
  information.
- **Right to Data Portability (Art. 20 GDPR)**: Receive your personal data in a structured, commonly
  used, machine-readable format.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR)**: Withdraw your consent for analytics or marketing
  at any time.
