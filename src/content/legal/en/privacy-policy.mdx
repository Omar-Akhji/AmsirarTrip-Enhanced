## 1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide when planning or booking an itinerary, contacting our team, or submitting an inquiry:

- Identity and contact details: full name, email address, telephone or WhatsApp number, and country of residence
- Booking specifications: travel dates, chosen tour routes, party size, dietary requirements, and custom requests
- Technical data: IP addresses, device types, and browser specifications recorded in server logs for security monitoring

We do not collect sensitive categories of personal data (such as racial background, political opinions, or religious beliefs).

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We process personal data in accordance with Moroccan Law No. 09-08 (CNDP) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for specific operational purposes:

- Fulfilling booking contracts: arranging private transport, mountain guides, desert camps, and riad reservations
- Customer communication: responding to route inquiries, sending pre-departure details, and coordinating via phone or WhatsApp
- Technical operations: reviewing server logs to troubleshoot site errors and maintain uptime
- Legal compliance: meeting Moroccan tourism licensing, tax, and transport reporting requirements

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal data to third parties.

## 3. Cookie Policy

Cookies are small text files stored on your computer or mobile device when you browse websites. They enable the website to remember your actions and preferences over time.

### Legal Bases for Cookies

Under EU GDPR (Regulation 2016/679) and the ePrivacy Directive (Directive 2002/58/EC):

- Necessary cookies are placed based on contractual necessity (Art. 6(1)(b)) and legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) to provide core services you request.
- Analytics and functional cookies are placed only with your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a)).

### Browser-Level Controls

Most browsers also allow you to block or delete cookies through browser settings:

- Chrome: Settings > Privacy and Security > Third-party cookies
- Safari: Settings > Privacy > Block all cookies
- Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
- Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies

## 4. Data Security & Storage

We maintain technical and operational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or disclosure:

- Encryption in transit: all web traffic uses TLS 1.3 encryption over HTTPS
- Content Security Policy: nonce-based script execution helps prevent cross-site scripting
- Access restrictions: access to traveler details is limited to personnel organizing your trip

## 5. Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following third-party infrastructure providers to support site operations:

- Hosting and VPS infrastructure: Hostinger Cloud VPS (European datacenters)
- Transactional email: Google Workspace for booking correspondence
- Maps: Google Maps API (loaded only upon visitor interaction)

## 6. Your Rights Under GDPR & Moroccan Law

Under Moroccan Law 09-08 and EU GDPR, you have the following rights regarding personal data:

- Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR): request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR): request correction of inaccurate records
- Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR): request deletion of personal information no longer required for legal or booking fulfillment
- Right to restrict processing (Art. 18 GDPR): request limits on how your data is used
- Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR): receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR): revoke consent for analytics at any time
