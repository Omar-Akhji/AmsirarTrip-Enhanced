## Overview

This three-day trip connects Marrakech to the high orange dunes of Erg Chebbi in Merzouga. You travel in a private, air-conditioned vehicle across the High Atlas Mountains via the Tizi n'Tichka pass (2,260m), stopping at the earthen ksar of Ait Ben Haddou and Ouarzazate before spending the first night in the Dades Valley.

On the second day, the road passes through Tinghir to reach the 300-meter limestone walls of Todra Gorges. From there, you continue past Erfoud and the Tafilalet palm groves to Merzouga. Late in the afternoon, you ride camels into the dunes for sunset and arrive at your desert camp for dinner, local music around the fire, and a quiet night under the stars.

After watching the sunrise over the sand and having breakfast, you drive back to Marrakech through Alnif, the palm-lined Draa Valley, and Ouarzazate, crossing the High Atlas again to arrive by early evening.

> **Pricing Note:** Includes private A/C transport, licensed driver-guide, 1 night in a Dades Valley riad/hotel, 1 night in a desert camp, dinners, daily breakfasts starting Day 2, and camel rides. Luxury camp upgrades with private bathrooms are available on request.

## Frequently Asked Questions

#### Q: Is this tour private or shared?
Most guests book this as a private tour, which lets you stop whenever you like for photos, coffee, or rest breaks. We also run small-group shared departures on select dates.

#### Q: How much driving is involved between Marrakech and Merzouga?
The round trip is roughly 1,120 km. Splitting it over three days keeps the pace manageable: Day 1 covers about 310 km to Dades Valley, Day 2 covers 250 km to Merzouga, and Day 3 returns via the Draa Valley and Ouarzazate.

#### Q: What are the desert camps like?

## Overview

This five-day itinerary focuses on Morocco's history, architecture, and coastal cities without long desert drives. Starting in Casablanca at the Hassan II Mosque, you follow the Atlantic coast to Rabat to explore the Kasbah of the Udayas and the 12th-century Hassan Tower.

Heading north into the Rif Mountains, you spend a night in Chefchaouen, famous for its blue-washed alleys and relaxed mountain atmosphere. From there, you visit the 2,000-year-old Roman ruins of Volubilis and the grand gates of imperial Meknes.

The trip concludes in Fes with a full-day guided walking tour through Fes el-Bali, exploring historic madrasas, artisan workshops, leather tanneries, and Al-Qarawiyyin University.

> **Pricing Note:** Includes private A/C vehicle with driver-guide, 4 nights in boutique riads/hotels (Casablanca, Chefchaouen, and 2 nights in Fes), a full-day licensed guide in Fes, airport transfers, and daily breakfasts from Day 2.

## Detailed Day-by-Day Itinerary

### Day 1: Arrival in Casablanca

- Pick-up from Casablanca Mohammed V Airport (CMN) or your city hotel.
- Visit the Hassan II Mosque, featuring a 210-meter minaret overlooking the Atlantic Ocean.
- Drive along the Ain Diab Corniche and pass through Casablanca's 1930s Art Deco district.
- Free evening to relax or try a local seafood restaurant.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Hotel in Casablanca
- **Meals Included:** None

### Day 2: Casablanca ➜ Rabat ➜ Chefchaouen

- After breakfast, drive up the coast to Rabat, Morocco’s capital.
- Visit the Hassan Tower, Mohammed V Mausoleum, and the blue-and-white streets of the Kasbah of the Udayas.
- Continue north into the Rif Mountains to reach Chefchaouen (~4 hours drive from Rabat).
- Evening free to stroll the blue alleys and main square.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Riad or Hotel in Chefchaouen
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast

### Day 3: Chefchaouen ➜ Volubilis ➜ Meknes ➜ Fes

- Morning free in Chefchaouen—optional walk up to the Spanish Mosque for views over the town.
- Depart for Volubilis to explore the preserved Roman ruins and intricate floor mosaics.
- Continue to Meknes to visit Bab Mansour Gate, El Hedim Square, and the Moulay Ismail Mausoleum.
- Arrive in Fes by early evening (~1 hour drive from Meknes).

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Riad or Hotel in Fes
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast

### Day 4: Full-Day Guided Tour of Fes

- Meet your licensed local guide after breakfast to explore Fes el-Bali.
- Visit Al-Qarawiyyin University (founded in 859 CE) and the Bou Inania Madrasa.
- See the Nejjarine Fountain and watch workers at the centuries-old Chouara Tanneries.
- Walk through the traditional souks selling brassware, ceramics, and leather goods.
- Free evening at your riad.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Accommodation:** Riad or Hotel in Fes
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast

### Day 5: Fes ➜ Departure Transfer

- Breakfast at your riad and free morning for last-minute shopping.
- Private transfer to Fes Saïss Airport (20 mins) or back to Casablanca Airport (~3.5 hours via highway) depending on your flight.

**Daily Logistics:**
- **Meals Included:** Breakfast

#### Q: Is this trip focused mostly on history and culture?
Yes. It is designed for travelers who want to explore Morocco’s imperial cities, Roman history, and artisan traditions without spending long days driving to the Sahara.

#### Q: Can we return to Casablanca on Day 5 instead of flying out of Fes?
Yes. On Day 5, your driver can take you straight back to Casablanca city or Mohammed V Airport via the highway (roughly 3.5 hours).

#### Q: How much time do we get in Chefchaouen?
You arrive in the afternoon on Day 2 and stay overnight, giving you both the quiet evening hours and the peaceful early morning before day-trip buses arrive.

#### Q: Is a local guide included for Fes?
Yes. A licensed local guide leads your walking tour through Fes el-Bali, covering the madrasas, university, tanneries, and historic markets.

#### Q: Are airport pick-ups and drop-offs included?
Yes. We pick you up at Casablanca Airport (CMN) or your hotel on Day 1, and drop you off at either Fes Airport (FEZ) or Casablanca Airport on Day 5.
